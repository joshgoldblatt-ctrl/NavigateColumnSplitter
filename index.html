Navigate File Generator for CUNY

Turn a Navigate enrollment export into an analysis-ready file: split any Assigned Staff relationship into its own column, and flag the categories you care about.

Version:1.2 Released:October 6, 2026 Developed by:Josh Goldblatt using Claude
v1.2 · October 6, 2026
  • Semester picker removed: upload, choose relationships, download. No Semester column in the export; file name is Navigate_File_<date>.csv.
v1.1 · October 5, 2026
  • Flexible column names: accepts Category or Categories, Student ID or EMPLID, and underscore variants, case-insensitive. The missing-column message now lists the headers found in the file.
v1.0 · October 2, 2026
  • Initial release. Campus-agnostic version of the LaGuardia Navigate Caseload Generator. Upload a Navigate enrollment export, pick the semester, choose which Assigned Staff relationships to split into columns, choose categories to flag, and export.
  • Relationship picker: relationship types are read from the file, not hard-coded, so any campus's structure works.
  • Derived columns: Academic Standing, Graduation Status, and Major are pulled from the Category field by prefix. A derived column that would clash with an existing column (usually Major) is suffixed "(from Category)".
